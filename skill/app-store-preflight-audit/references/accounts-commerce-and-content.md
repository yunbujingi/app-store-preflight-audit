# Accounts, commerce, UGC, and regulated content

Read only sections activated by the product map.

## Accounts and login

Trace registration, sign-in, guest access, sign-out, password recovery, credential revocation, account deletion, and deletion of local/cloud/third-party data. Distinguish account deletion from deactivation or support contact.

When third-party or social login exists, retrieve the current App Review login-services rule and evaluate its exceptions. Do not decide compliance solely from presence or absence of `AuthenticationServices`.

Review credentials, OTP, invite, geography, enterprise identity, and backend prerequisites belong in review accessibility and notes.

## Commerce

Classify what is sold before applying payment rules:

- digital feature/content/service consumed in the app;
- physical goods or services consumed outside the app;
- reader/multiplatform access;
- person-to-person service;
- donation/tip;
- regulated financial or crypto function;
- advertising or enterprise access.

Then check storefront-specific current rules, entitlements, IAP visibility, product metadata, purchase states, cancellation, pending, restore, entitlement reconciliation, family sharing where applicable, subscription disclosures, trials, price accuracy, and reviewer accessibility.

Do not label an external link noncompliant without recording storefront and applicable exception.

## UGC and communications

Determine whether content is private to one user, shared with selected people, public, or enables direct communication. Apply moderation/report/block/contact requirements only when the actual interaction falls within the current rule. Verify abuse handling and reviewer reachability rather than inferring from the word “share.”

## Sensitive and regulated domains

For health, medical, finance, gambling, children, VPN/MDM, legal, safety, or government identity features, load current product-specific Apple rules and applicable law assumptions. Mark legal conclusions for qualified human review; this skill does not provide legal advice.
